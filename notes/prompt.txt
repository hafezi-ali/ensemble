THEORY TO GROUND EACH DESIGN (cite, do not assert)
================================================================================
- GMM vs K-means: the full covariance Sigma_k models non-spherical and rotated clusters and
  yields a soft posterior P(C_k|x) usable directly as a fusion weight; K-means is the hard,
  isotropic, equal-covariance special case. Duda, Hart & Stork (2001), Ch. 3 (Gaussian
  discriminants) and Ch. 10 (mixture densities, EM, K-means as a limiting case). This is the
  actual refutation of "GMM is just soft K-means" -- the containment goes the other way.
- Density-weighted fusion vs DELAK's distance-weighted fusion: Guo et al. (2021).
- Dynamic selection and region of competence: Ko et al. (2008); Cruz et al. (2018).
- Drift protocol and prequential evaluation: Gomes et al. (2017); Ditzler et al. (2015).
- High-dimensional GMM concern (raised by referees): answer it directly with the PCA variance
  retention, the covariance type (diagonal or regularised) and its selection criterion, and
  the parameter count per cluster at the retained dimensionality. Do not dismiss the concern.

Verify every new citation with the research connectors before adding it to
manuscript/references.bib. Do not invent references, volumes or page numbers.
